Wasteland RTS — Windows playable (Oct 17–19)

How to launch
1. Unzip this archive. Keep the files in the same folder. The game looks for the data folder beside Wasteland RTS.exe.
2. On 64-bit Windows, double-click Wasteland RTS.exe.
3. You play the Remnant at Goldendale. The AI plays the Homestead Compact at Hood River.

Controls
- Pan: W A S D, or the arrow keys. Middle mouse drag, or right mouse drag, also pans.
- Zoom: mouse wheel.
- Select: left click on the map. The top strip of the HUD does not select.
- Order: right click, without dragging.
- Research Tier II: the research button on the left HUD.
- Build depot and Raise: buttons on the left HUD.
- Under attack: the button jumps the camera to the alert when it is shown.
- Speed: 1x, 4x, 16x, or max.

Win condition
Capture the enemy headquarters.
Lose your own headquarters and you lose.
